\Textcite{shannon1948mathematical} defined the entropy of a discrete source as
\begin{equation}
  H(X) = -\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_i \log_2 p_i ,
  \label{eq:entropy}
\end{equation}
where $p_i$ is the probability of the $i$-th symbol; \cref{eq:entropy} is
maximal for equiprobable symbols.
